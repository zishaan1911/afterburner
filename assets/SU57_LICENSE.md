OpenFlightSim's adaptation converts the model to metres, narrows its span by 3 %
to the published 14.1 m, sets the undercarriage to a static stance, groups its
existing control surfaces, nozzles, doors and gear under animation pivots, cuts
each fin blade from its root fairing, replaces the glazing materials and adds
three reduced levels of detail. No geometry or texture artwork was added.

The four installed `assets/aircraft/su57/su57_lod*.glb` files are that
adaptation and are offered under the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license. They are
distributed unencrypted: the license does not allow technical measures that
